About Architique

Architique is a premium marble and interior design company dedicated to transforming homes, villas, offices, and commercial spaces into elegant masterpieces. We specialize in luxury marble flooring, CNC marble designs, custom marble patterns, wall cladding, staircases, kitchens, bathrooms, TV walls, feature walls, and decorative stone installations. Our experienced team combines expert craftsmanship with high-quality materials to create timeless and durable designs that reflect style, luxury, and perfection.

At Architique, every project is completed with precision and attention to detail. Whether you are building a new home or renovating an existing space, we provide complete marble solutions—from design consultation to final installation. Our goal is to deliver outstanding quality, modern aesthetics, and customer satisfaction in every project we undertake.
